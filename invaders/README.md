# Space Invaders (Scratch)

A simple Space Invaders game, generated by `build.mjs` through `../turbowarp-remote/twctl.mjs`.
`space-invaders.sb3` is the exported result and opens in TurboWarp or Scratch.

- Arrow keys move the ship, space shoots (one bullet at a time).
- 32 invaders (4 x 8 clones) march sideways, drop at the edges and speed up as they die.
- Win by clearing them all; lose if they reach the bottom or touch the ship. The score shows on stage.

Rebuild: `open -n ../turbowarp-remote/TurboWarpRemote.app && node build.mjs`
